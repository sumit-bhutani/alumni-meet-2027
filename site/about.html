About

The story behind
Jashn-e-Yaarana

Why we are coming back together, and what the name means.

The name

Celebration and friendship, in one phrase.

Jashncelebration
Yaaranafriendship

DC26 in Washington brought more than 300 of us together, and the question at the end was how to stay connected. This year's answer is a reunion built around the yaar: the batchmate from your hostel, the senior who got you your first job, the junior you now mentor.

Bring them, find them, or meet them for the first time.

Two lineages, one table

The names changed. The people kept finding each other.

Route one

DCE → DTU

Delhi College of Engineering, which began as Delhi Polytechnic in 1941, became Delhi Technological University in 2009.

Route two

DIT → NSIT → NSUT

Delhi Institute of Technology, then NSIT, now Netaji Subhas University of Technology.

Not just to survive. To soar.

Four ideas the reunion is built on.

Courage

Stories from alumni who took the leap: new countries, companies and careers.

Inclusion

Every batch, branch and ability. Families welcome at every event.

Opportunity

Jobs, mentorship and introductions shared freely across the network.

Together

One community, from Kashmere Gate to Bawana Road to Dwarka.
