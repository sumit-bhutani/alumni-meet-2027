Our vision

Decades of friends, joined by one alma mater.

Jashncelebration
Yaaranafriendship

Jashn-e-Yaarana is a celebration of friendship. It brings together alumni of Delhi Polytechnic, DCE, DIT, DTU, NSIT and NSUT, from the earliest batches to the most recent, who walked the same campuses decades apart and left with friends for life.

Our vision is a gathering where every batch has a place at the table: classmates who have not met in thirty years, seniors and juniors who never overlapped but share the same hostel stories, and a new generation meeting the community their parents belong to.

The alma mater is the thread that ties it together. Jashn-e-Yaarana is how we celebrate it, and how we make sure the friendships it began carry on for generations to come.

Route one

Delhi Polytechnic began in 1941, grew into Delhi College of Engineering, and became Delhi Technological University in 2009.

Route two
